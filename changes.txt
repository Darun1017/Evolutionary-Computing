1. Fix the MIN_KEEP Population Freezing (Immediate Impact)

In select_and_reproduce (lines 221 & 822):

python
MIN_KEEP = 10
min_keep = min(MIN_KEEP, target_size)
The Problem: When you ran with --pop-size 10, min_keep was min(10, 10) = 10. Because min_keep = 10, the survivor list final held all 10 old individuals, leaving 0 slots for mutate_fn() to generate new offspring. The population was essentially frozen and barely produced new mutations. (Standard CCEA, by contrast, kept only 1 elite and generated 9 new mutated children every single generation).
The Fix: Make min_keep proportional to population size (e.g., keeping only the top 15%–20% as elites/survivors, and generating 80%–85% new offspring):
python
min_keep = max(1, int(0.20 * target_size))  # Keep top 2 when pop=10
